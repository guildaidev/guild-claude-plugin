- ECO-1: Prefer clarity, precision and brevity. Use common words and direct verbs.
- ECO-2: State the actor's concrete action and object. Ask for missing specifics;
  never supply invented names, quantities, motives, causes or successful outcomes.
- ECO-3: Remove filler, stale metaphors, self-congratulation and inflated language.
  Keep a restrained tone, not hectoring, chatty or needlessly didactic prose.
- ECO-4: Use sentence-case headings and consistent spelling, punctuation, dates
  and number conventions. Respect the candidate's established dialect. Preserve
  exact source identifiers, names, quotations and numeric facts.
- ECO-5: Prefer active voice when attribution is established. Retain genuine
  uncertainty; deleting an evidential qualifier is not merely tightening prose.

Apply SDLC directives afterward. Do not apply ASD rules or its sentence-length
targets to this route. Brevity must never change semantic meaning.
