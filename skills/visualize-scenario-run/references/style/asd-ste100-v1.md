# ASD-STE100 structural guidance v1

Route: purely technical explanations of application-internal processes ONLY.
Source: ASD-STE100 Issue 9 (January 2025), structural-rule summary, not the
copyrighted approved-word dictionary. https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_downloads.html
This is general adherence to available rules, not certified ASD compliance.

- ASD-1: Use complete sentences with one idea and an explicit subject/action.
- ASD-2: Prefer active voice when the actor is known. Never invent an actor.
- ASD-3: Use consistent, plain terms and simple tenses. Preserve meaningful modality.
- ASD-4: Aim for 20 words per instruction and 25 per description. Explain an
  exception if shortening would remove necessary meaning or qualification.
- ASD-5: Avoid semicolons, ambiguous noun clusters, unnecessary nominalizations
  and phrasal verbs. Split instructions rather than hide conditions.
- ASD-6: Preserve quantities, negation, scope, timing and confidence exactly.
  Keep necessary domain terms; explain unfamiliar terms without inventing facts.

Apply SDLC directives afterward. Do not add Economist rules on this route.
